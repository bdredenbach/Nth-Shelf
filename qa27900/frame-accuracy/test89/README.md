# Test89 — neighbor-witnessed page-edge scenes

The new route is an optional, append-only supplement. It uses the retained ragged-gutter candidate generator at independent seed radii 4 and 6, requires contour agreement, and accepts only a tall scene with one horizontal and one vertical physical page edge. A validated short neighboring frame must share at least 65% of its shorter vertical span and lie within 2% of the page width. Every source-pixel owner remains exclusive.

The retained boundary evidence is unchanged for ordinary samples. Only a corner remainder of at most eight samples on a side that is at least 95% physical image edge may rely on complete mixed ink/paper support. This does not allow arbitrary missing gutters. The original internal-divider and enclosed-inset checks remain.

The reader retains proof 41's exact contour and skips legacy spill expansion. Cache and Android identities advance to 2.79.89 / 28017.

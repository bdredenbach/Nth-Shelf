# Test73: rooted shared-boundary completion

Run `bash qa27900/frame-accuracy/test73/run-retained.sh` for the retained Node
suite plus the new contract and cache checks. The previous Test71 contract is
unchanged and must still pass. The compressed fixture contains geometry and
proof evidence only, never comic artwork.

The generator admits radius 8 only for its explicit cooperative candidate API.
Prior paths keep their earlier settings. The new optional module runs after the
accepted Test71 supplement and appends validated additions only. It does not
publish the untested Test72 replacement module.

For each candidate, two source scales must agree after subtracting already
accepted ownership. Disagreement is limited to 0.2% of the union, no more than
0.035% of the page, and a three-analysis-pixel boundary band. At most 2.5% of a
source candidate may already belong to accepted owners. The final consensus is
reconstructed and validated from the stored source proofs and blockers. It must
have one complete contour, no page-edge clipping, and no substantial unresolved
internal divider. The fixed three-pixel page margin is not required when the
actual contour is still enclosed.

A boundary is witnessed by exterior-paper rays to one or several accepted
neighbors. Chains are limited to three rooted levels; candidates cannot justify
themselves or form unsupported cycles. Previous contours and metadata are never
modified. The new proof is version 29, `boundary-stable-rooted-paper-network`.

The contract covers radius-8 generation/validation, 2x2 rooted chaining, a wide
panel collectively supported by three smaller neighbors, a nonwhite separator,
empty and complete maps, 27 malformed/cyclic proof rejections, and geometry/spill
binding. Source-image comparisons and actual reader results are recorded in the
adjacent JSON files. Original comic artwork is not rerun by CI without the user
archive.

Local original-image results: 91 pages; 89 maps exactly unchanged; all 150 prior
descriptors preserved; four additions across two pages. On issue 3 reader9/23,
three existing selections become six. Issue 1 reader19/24 gains its lower-left
scene. No other page changes. Counts alone do not prove correctness of unresolved
layouts. The native Chromium touch harness used a minimal offline DOM, not a
physical Android device.

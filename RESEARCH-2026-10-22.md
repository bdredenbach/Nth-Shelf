## Status

Research only. The qualified detached-caption candidate is unchanged. Source173 remains the accepted baseline; no additional pop-out, APK, Git publication or promotion is claimed here. Private source artwork, reference masks and exact tap receipts remain outside public Git.

The original Test179 issue 3 inventory had 12 incomplete outputs, four contaminated outputs, four misses and three ownership-review cases. The caption repair resolves one incomplete output. ## What has been established

- A source-only planar ink graph can preserve digital topology, explicitly account for filled skeleton cells and nested boundary loops, and map its free faces back to source background regions.
- On the printed front frame under investigation, a bounded observed-edge winding retains all independently required reference samples and excludes all confidently foreign samples. This is an interior/centerline result, not a complete raster outline or ownership certificate.
- Removing the finite graph-edge search support yields a larger enclosure that includes neighboring artwork. The desired frame is not the largest full-source biconnected block.
- The difference between the two observed source cycles forms a separate simple, closed neighboring cycle. It contains the disputed glow region and the neighboring recorded test tap, while excluding the front-frame tap. No artificial closing edge was introduced. Test taps are diagnostic inputs, not production geometry.

## Negative evidence preserved

Fourteen independent controls were frozen before generic proposal evaluation. The unchanged source-layout nominator proposed seven cases; only three produced winding hypotheses. All three omit some required border ink, and the nested-scene case includes foreign content. The remaining cases are no-nomination or graph-rejection outcomes, not successful repairs. No crop was installed.

A fixed support band can interrupt valid rounded or variable-width outlines. Enclosure alone cannot distinguish a scene from an internal picture or inset. Keeping every connected unknown ink pixel preserves genuine antenna-like details but also preserves foreign native stems. None of these shortcuts is accepted.

A full-source two-sided face/junction diagnostic finds eleven external branches attached to the proposed frame. Nine have the same source face on both sides; two border a face intersecting both current canonical contours. The predeclared ownership rule assigns no attached branch uniquely to another scene and abstains. T-shaped junctions or lack of an interior continuation are not, alone, ownership certificates.

## The upstream ownership problem

The final contour conflict is not evidence of two independently trusted owner anchors. The detector discarded its transient pre-growth component correspondence. A separately instrumented replay recovered those components, with byte-identical original/instrumented output and exact parent-proof matches. Both pre-growth components already contain parts of the disputed source face. The contamination therefore predates label growth; recovering metadata does not resolve it.

The next model must derive scene ownership from source cells and independently justified context, rather than using the old contaminated contours or cores as authoritative labels. Do not replace this gap with majority overlap, a handpicked native coordinate, a widened search band, or a forced ownership assignment.

## Cost and qualification limits

The generic native winding proposal takes about 4.15 seconds and 318 MiB peak RSS in the research environment. The full-page face/junction audit takes about 26.94 seconds and 738 MiB after an exact lookup optimization. These are not Reader or phone measurements. The full-page diagnostic has separately declared larger analysis caps; it does not silently expand the generic proposal's limits.

Independent implementation review checked 65,536 small graph/cell configurations and 512 thinning configurations, plus branch and loop fixtures. Observed-edge, topology and immutable-input checks passed. The reusable research wrapper still needs robust input validation, resource-error handling and deadline isolation. Evaluation used fresh externally timed processes. No production JavaScript integration, full native regression of a new repair, browser/compositor test or Android qualification has occurred.

## Bounded ambiguity audit

The frozen 11-chain binary model enumerated all 2,048 assignments. All remain feasible because the retained graph-only observations supply no trusted semantic anchor. Every chain remains unknown. Paired foreground-spur and foreign/behind-frame interpretations preserve the same observed geometry. This is a transparent missing-constraint result, not a learned detector or proof that the full artwork is inherently ambiguous. Face-wide ownership is not assumed; within-chain changes and shared junction ownership remain outside this coarse model.

Constructed provenance-based anchor tests, contradictory-anchor rejection, graph-ID/coordinate/variable-order invariance and unchanged source-input hashes pass. An initial graph-walk/source-face ID mismatch stopped before enumeration, was documented and corrected using the existing exact mapping, then re-frozen. No source pixels or production code changed.

## Next bounded design

Add independently evaluated appearance/scene context to the source-cell geometry. Freeze a small multi-title diagnostic corpus, clear interior/entity references and explicit unknown bands. Compare graph-only proposals with probabilistic scene anchors and prompt-conditioned masks. Keep oracle-assisted prompt diagnostics separate from automatic performance. Measure contamination, true-protrusion loss, coverage and abstention; reserve untouched titles for evaluation. A small pilot can reject approaches, not establish production-level low error rates. This does not make mathematical certainty a requirement for every practical detector. A learned appearance/context classifier with independently measured errors, calibration and abstention is a legitimate next model, but no such classifier has been trained or qualified here.

Two concrete model classes were reviewed, not downloaded or executed. [Magi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10224) supplies manga-oriented entity boxes and associations, not pixel ownership; its roughly 0.5B-parameter model and licensing need separate deployment review. [MobileSAM](https://github.com/ChaoningZhang/MobileSAM) is a smaller prompted-mask model, not a source of trustworthy scene prompts. Its reported 9.66M parameters do not establish full Android/WebView compatibility or phone timing. Export, activation memory, offline cold start and calibrated errors must be measured separately before integration.

Primary research informing that design:

- [Hoiem et al., Recovering Occlusion Boundaries from a Single Image](https://dhoiem.cs.illinois.edu/publications/iccv07hoiem.pdf): directed boundary/region ownership and junction consistency; natural-image classifiers are not directly transferable to line art.
- [Stochastic completion fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.11322): possible hidden contour continuations, not proof of semantic ownership.
- [QPBO partial labeling](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2007/06/cvpr07-QPBOpi.pdf): leaving variables unresolved rather than forcing a label; guarantees concern the specified energy, not the correctness of its semantic assumptions.

Any eventual repair still requires independent negatives, immutable real-source regressions, performance measurements and separate browser/phone qualification. The four landscape-title misses, other ownership defects and first/nearby-tap cost remain unresolved work.
